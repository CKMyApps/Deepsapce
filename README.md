# Deepsapce
Deepspace is a retro, turn-based space combat game from CK My Apps, inspired by the 1976 BASIC original. Choose a patrol system, pick your ship and loadout, and duel an enemy vessel. Close in for stronger shots, pull back to protect your hull, or disengage. Play in your browser with green CRT visuals, sci-fi chiptune, and local save and load.
